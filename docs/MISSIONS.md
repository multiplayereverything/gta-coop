## How missions work in coop
- The **host** (first player on the server) runs the mission script. Other players are partners.
- **Ported** missions (upstream CoopAndreas) have per-player objectives, blips, checkpoints and teleports
  (`Coop.*` commands in the script). The coop trigger needs **all players in the marker** for ported starts.
- **Unported** missions show "This mission is currently unsupported" and run host-driven. Our fork adds a fallback
  (`COpCodeSync.cpp` IsUnportedMissionFallbackOpcode): their texts, help, pass/fail banners, mission text table,
  fades and cutscenes are sent to every player, so partners can follow along and vote to skip cutscenes.
  Partner-specific objectives, blips that only show for the host, checkpoints and the features tagged below are not synced.
- Cutscene skip is a vote: it needs **every** player (4/4).

## Feature tags (not synced or partly synced, see AGENTS.md "Sync backlog")
**Since 2026-10-25** partners also get, in host-run missions: mission pickups, fires, "go here" markers, race
checkpoints, mission objects, trains, trailers and the money reward. The tags below were recorded before that; still
not shared: `wanted` (per player on purpose), `gang-group` (mission gang members follow the host), `gang-wars`,
`parachute`, `jetpack`.
`pickups` mission pickups exist only for the host | `wanted` wanted level is per player | `money` rewards/costs go to the host
| `fire` script fires local | `trains` no train sync | `trailers` no trailer sync | `gang-group` recruits follow the host only
| `gang-wars` territory wars unsynced | `checkpoints` race checkpoints host-only in unported missions | `parachute`, `jetpack`
| `aircraft` flying works but is the least tested | `interiors` partners must enter the same interior themselves
